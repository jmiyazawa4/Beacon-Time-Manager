# Daily KJV passage selections

The owner requested encouragement, peace, faith, and perseverance. These 80 passages rotate through the year separately from the yearly reading plan. February 29 uses Psalm 118:24.

To refine the selection, name a reference to keep or remove, suggest a replacement, or say which themes you want more often. For example: “More like Philippians 4:6–7; remove [reference].” Whole selected passages are quoted from the pinned KJV source in `daily-verses.json`.
